# Content and media

The app uses original short editorial summaries with links to further reading. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by these organizations.

## Learning sources

- [RSPCA: dog body language](https://nextgen.rspca.org.uk/kindness-hub/looking-after-dogs/dog-body-language)
- [RSPCA: toilet training](https://science.rspca.org.uk/en/web/rspca/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/training/toilettraining)
- [RSPCA: dog training](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/training)
- [Dogs Trust: reward-based training](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/techniques/positive-reinforcement-training-with-rewards)

## Breed further reading

- [Golden Retriever](https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/retriever-golden/)
- [Border Collie](https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/border-collie/)
- [Beagle](https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/beagle/)
- [Poodle](https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/poodle-standard/)
- [Labrador Retriever](https://www.akc.org/dog-breeds/labrador-retriever/)

AKC group labels are used for the pedigree guides. Poodles span Non-sporting (Standard/Miniature) and Toy (Toy). Mixed-breed dogs are listed as individuals, not an AKC breed.
